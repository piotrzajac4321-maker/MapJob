# Anatomia promptu — 12 warstw (z gotowymi frazami EN)

Buduj prompt sekwencyjnie. Każda warstwa = 1–2 frazy. Łącz przecinkami w jeden ciąg. Frazy poniżej to „klocki" — wybieraj i sklejaj.

---

## 1. Subject + identity lock

**Tryb A (na zdjęciu klienta) — ZAWSZE zaczynaj od:**
> Generate a photorealistic newborn baby based on the uploaded reference photo. Preserve the baby's face, head shape, skin tone, ear shape and peaceful expression with 100% fidelity — do not alter, beautify, slim, reshape or age any facial feature; keep the identity identical to the reference.

**Tryb B (fikcyjny model):**
> Photorealistic newborn baby, a fictional infant not based on any real person, natural newborn head shape and peaceful sleeping expression.

## 2. Wiek i etap (poza ogólna)

- Newborn (5–21 dni): `curled-up sleeping newborn, knees tucked toward the chest, natural newborn proportions`
- Sitter (6–9 mies.): `sitting upright unassisted, chubby cheeks, alert curious expression`
- Toddler: `standing/sitting toddler, playful natural posture`

## 3. Wardrobe (ubranko — materiał + splot + kolor + krój)

Opisuj **fakturę**, nie tylko kolor:
> Outfit: soft cream chunky-knit ribbed romper with a matching knotted bonnet, hand-knitted wool texture, gentle natural folds.

Klocki materiałów: `chunky-knit / ribbed-knit / waffle-knit / merino wool / brushed cotton / muslin wrap / lace-trimmed gown / velvet / organic linen`.

## 4. Poza i dłonie (krytyczne dla realizmu)

> Arms: both hands resting softly together near the chin, fingers gently curled, **anatomically correct hands with exactly five fingers each**, natural newborn skin folds at the wrists.

Bezpieczne pozy: `hands near the face, classic newborn pose` / `arms resting down by the body` / `one hand under the cheek`. Unikaj nienaturalnie wyprostowanych palców.

## 5. Set / tło (warstwy + głębia + materiały)

> Background: a layered neutral cream studio setup — a soft brushed-cotton beanbag in front, draped muslin folds behind, smooth out-of-focus backdrop in the far distance, clear sense of depth.

Zawsze podaj **3 plany**: na czym leży dziecko → co tuż za nim → dalekie tło (rozmyte).

## 6. Props (faktura, nie nazwa)

> Props: a vintage woven wicker basket lined with a thick cream faux-fur layer, a small dried eucalyptus sprig beside it, all softly textured.

## 7. Światło (najważniejsza warstwa realizmu)

> Lighting: a single large softbox as key light positioned high and slightly to the left, soft directional falloff, gentle 2:1 lighting ratio, delicate shadows under the chin, **soft catchlights in the eyes**, warm 4800K color temperature.

Warianty: `large window light from the side` / `golden-hour warm glow` / `dramatic low-key single side light` / `soft even high-key studio light`.

> **Catchlights w oczach** = sygnał „to prawdziwe zdjęcie". Dodawaj zawsze, gdy oczy widoczne.

## 8. Kamera i obiektyw

> Shot on a full-frame camera with an 85mm prime lens at f/2.0, eye-level angle, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, subject tack-sharp on the eyes.

Klocki: `85mm f/2.0` (portret/detal), `50mm f/1.8` (szerszy kadr), `35mm` (rodzinne/scena). DOF: `shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh`.

## 9. Sygnał realizmu (anty-„plastik")

> Photorealistic newborn skin with fine natural texture, visible pores, soft peach fuzz on the cheeks, subtle realistic redness and skin mottling, natural subsurface scattering, gentle photographic film grain, no digital smoothing.

## 10. Color grade

> Color: warm neutral palette, accurate white balance, soft creamy midtones, muted pastel accents, true-to-life skin tones, gentle filmic contrast.

Warianty: `airy bright pastel` / `warm earthy boho` / `rich low-key premium` / `clean modern fine-art neutral`.

## 11. Nastrój

> Mood: peaceful, tender, calm, premium fine-art newborn photography atmosphere.

## 12. Technikalia + negatywy

> High detail, professional newborn photography, ultra-realistic. Aspect ratio 3:4.

**Negatywy (dla modeli wspierających, albo jako fraza „avoid"):**
> Avoid: plastic or waxy skin, over-smoothing, oversharpening, HDR look, extra or missing fingers, distorted or fused hands, deformed ears, asymmetric eyes, text, watermark, logo, harsh artificial lighting, cartoonish look.
