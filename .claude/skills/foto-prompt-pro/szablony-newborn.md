# Szablony newborn — 10 stylów studyjnych (wersja PRO)

Rozbudowane wersje 10 stylów z `studio-ai/docs/PROMPTY.md` — każdy przeszedł przez 12 warstw. Wszystkie w **Trybie A** zaczynają od identity-locka; do reklam (Tryb B) podmień warstwę 1 na frazę z `anatomia-promptu.md`.

**Prefiks Tryb A (wklej przed każdym szablonem):**
> Generate a photorealistic newborn baby based on the uploaded reference photo. Preserve the baby's face, head shape, skin tone, ear shape and peaceful expression with 100% fidelity — do not alter or beautify any facial feature, keep the identity identical to the reference.

**Sufiks realizmu (wklej po każdym szablonie):**
> Photorealistic newborn skin with fine texture, visible pores, soft peach fuzz, subtle natural redness, subsurface scattering, gentle film grain, no digital smoothing. Anatomically correct hands with five fingers each. Shot on a full-frame camera, 85mm at f/2.0, eye-level, shallow depth of field, creamy bokeh, tack-sharp eyes, soft catchlights. High detail, ultra-realistic. Aspect ratio 3:4. Avoid: plastic skin, over-smoothing, extra fingers, distorted hands, text, watermark, harsh lighting.

---

### 1. Klasyczne studio (domyślny, najbezpieczniejszy)
`Outfit: soft cream chunky-knit romper and matching knotted bonnet, hand-knitted texture. Arms: both arms resting softly down by the body, relaxed newborn pose. Background: seamless warm cream studio backdrop, soft brushed-cotton beanbag, smooth out-of-focus depth. Lighting: large softbox key light high and slightly left, gentle 2:1 ratio, soft chin shadow, warm 4800K. Color: warm neutral palette, true-to-life skin. Mood: timeless, elegant, fine-art.`

### 2. Boho / naturalne
`Outfit: oatmeal ribbed-knit romper with a soft muslin wrap. Arms: hands gently near the cheeks. Background: layered textured beige muslin, dried pampas grass and eucalyptus accents, earthy out-of-focus backdrop, clear depth. Props: woven basket lined with cream faux-fur. Lighting: large window light from the left, soft falloff, warm 4900K. Color: warm earthy boho palette. Mood: organic, tender.`

### 3. Zima / Boże Narodzenie
`Outfit: tiny red-and-white knitted santa outfit with a little pompom hat. Arms: resting down by the body. Background: soft white faux-fur blanket, warm bokeh fairy lights in the far distance, cozy depth. Lighting: warm key light plus golden bokeh highlights, 3800K cozy tone. Color: warm golden festive palette. Mood: cozy, magical.`

### 4. Wiosna / kwiaty
`Outfit: delicate pastel floral romper with a tiny flower headband. Arms: hands gently up near the face. Background: a soft nest of fresh pastel flowers and greenery, the baby resting in a floral wreath, airy out-of-focus garden behind. Lighting: bright airy daylight, soft high-key, 5500K. Color: fresh delicate pastel palette. Mood: light, joyful.`

### 5. Bajkowy księżyc
`Outfit: soft pale-grey star-patterned onesie. Arms: resting down by the body. Background: the baby sleeping on a crescent-moon prop wrapped in soft clouds, deep navy starry backdrop with tiny glowing stars, dreamy depth. Lighting: soft dreamy glow from above, cool 4200K with warm rim. Color: deep navy with soft silver accents. Mood: dreamy, fantasy newborn art.`

### 6. Leśny / zwierzątka
`Outfit: a knitted animal-ear bonnet (bear or fox) and cozy earth-tone romper. Arms: hands near the face. Background: a cozy mossy woodland nest, soft autumn leaves, blurred forest with light filtering through behind. Lighting: warm dappled forest light, 4600K. Color: warm forest earth tones. Mood: snug, whimsical.`

### 7. Minimalistyczny beż (fine-art)
`Outfit: simple undyed cotton wrap, minimal. Arms: resting down by the body. Background: seamless warm taupe backdrop, no props, lots of negative space. Lighting: a single soft side light, gentle gradient falloff, 4700K. Color: ultra-clean modern neutral. Mood: calm, editorial fine-art.`

### 8. Vintage koronka
`Outfit: soft ivory lace-trimmed gown. Arms: hands gently near the face. Background: an antique lace blanket in a wooden basket, soft sepia-warm tones, blurred vintage interior behind. Lighting: soft warm directional light, 4000K, nostalgic. Color: warm sepia, film-like. Mood: nostalgic, classic.`

### 9. Granatowy aksamit (premium low-key)
`Outfit: cream knitted romper for contrast. Arms: resting down by the body. Background: deep navy velvet drape, baby on a soft cream layer, rich dark depth. Lighting: dramatic low-key single side light, strong falloff into shadow, 4300K. Color: rich premium low-key, deep contrast. Mood: elegant, luxurious.`

### 10. Pastelowy róż / błękit (dziewczynka / chłopiec)
`Outfit: matching pastel knitted wrap (soft pink for a girl / soft blue for a boy). Arms: resting down by the body. Background: smooth seamless pastel backdrop matching the wrap, soft even depth. Lighting: soft even high-key studio light, 5200K. Color: gentle pastel, clean. Mood: sweet, classic.`
